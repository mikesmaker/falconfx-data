### Core Engineering Requirements for `booster.py`

#### 1. Pre-Checkout Flash Trigger & Grid Ingestion
- Create an ingestion engine that simulates or processes early-warning indicators from external delivery platforms (e.g., shopping cart footprint spikes, active user localization, restaurant app traffic surges).
- Project these live demand signals onto a virtual 110m x 110m coordinate grid matrix generated by rounding the parsed latitudes and longitudes from `places.json` to 3 decimal places.
- Identify impending checkout explosions 3 to 5 minutes BEFORE primary platforms like Bolt or Yango officially trigger visible driver surge pricing.

#### 2. Predictive Velocity Wave Calculation
- Accept active rider telemetry inputs: current `lat`, `lng`, `speed` (km/h), and `heading` (direction vector).
- Compute the rider's Time to Arrival (TTA) for all surrounding high-yield grid squares.
- Map the demand wave as a moving curve, calculating target vectors so the rider enters the grid tile exactly as the pre-checkout phase transitions into an active dispatch ping.

#### 3. Real-World Operational Refinements
- **Kitchen Prep Buffer:** Apply category-specific padding. Fast-food or retail items are marked as instant velocity targets. Local chop bars and heavy kitchens receive a automated 12-to-15 minute preparation delay so riders don't sit idle waiting for food to cook.
- **Traffic Friction Multiplier:** Incorporate a mathematical degradation factor for travel speed based on known Accra bottleneck locations (e.g., Spintex Road, Tetteh Quarshie, Kwame Nkrumah Circle) relative to the time of day.
- **Leapfrog Sequential Routing:** Implement predictive chaining logic. While a rider is traveling along a corridor to drop off a current delivery, the engine must automatically begin scanning and caching pre-checkout triggers concentrated around their *intended drop-off destination* to guarantee back-to-back earnings.

#### 4. Advanced "Outside-the-Box" African Megacity Layers
- **Return Ticket Arbitrage:** Track deliveries going to low-density residential boundaries (e.g., Adenta, Kasoa, Weija). Lock in the destination coordinates 30 minutes prior to arrival and prioritize searching for B2B waybill packages or wholesale distribution runs in those perimeters that need transport back to the commercial core.

Trigger localized "Booster Clusters" to intercept wholesale traders needing high-paying multi-package distribution runs the moment the bus luggage compartments open.
- **Monsoon Micro-Climate Multiplier:** Monitor live weather variations. If a rain block paralyzes one district, calculate the coordinates of the moving "dry edge" boundary to actively nudge riders toward nearby high-demand dry zones experiencing extreme courier deficits.
